# ACE Goals and Success Definition

## Product goal

ACE should function as a reliable external executive system that reduces the number of cognitive operations the operator must repeatedly perform.

It should remember broadly, display narrowly, interrupt rarely, and ask only consequential questions.

## Co-equal P0 goals

### P0-A - Generic productivity and execution reliability

ACE must help the operator:

- capture ordinary work without classification labor;
- close routine tasks and recurring duties reliably;
- track promises, follow-ups, and waiting items;
- reduce forgotten commitments and trust debt;
- resume interrupted work quickly;
- coordinate without continuous mental monitoring;
- operate effectively during fragmented professional weeks.

### P0-B - Personal conversion and career compounding

ACE must help the operator:

- protect original research, building, and writing time;
- define and ship small evidence units;
- distinguish project motion from operator-created evidence;
- preserve durable outputs beyond individual sessions;
- support publication, external review, reuse, and adoption;
- transfer operational ownership without losing provenance;
- increase the share of work that creates future autonomy.

Neither goal may be optimized by materially degrading the other.

## Cognitive operations to off-load

ACE should remove repeated human effort in:

- remembering;
- reconstructing context;
- monitoring deadlines and replies;
- repeatedly prioritizing unchanged work;
- translating meetings into actions;
- checking whether work actually closed;
- assembling context for agents;
- tracking who owns what;
- identifying whether work is shipping or only moving.

## Final user experience goal

The operator should normally:

1. start a scoped Cowork or agent panel;
2. perform the work with selected context;
3. approve a compact exit package;
4. inspect durable state in Obsidian when needed;
5. trust scheduled loops to return work at the correct time.

The operator should not need to operate a CLI, maintain a knowledge graph, reorganize the whole filesystem, or supervise continuous agents.

## Non-goals

ACE is not:

- a comprehensive archive of everything read or discussed;
- a replacement for institutional clinical systems;
- a universal file crawler;
- an autonomous career decision-maker;
- a large plugin platform;
- a permanent personality model;
- a system that rewards task volume;
- a requirement to migrate all existing notes;
- a mechanism for moving PHI into personal tools.

## Success after 30 days

- no acknowledged commitment is forgotten;
- ordinary task closure is not worse than baseline;
- at least one protected block is preserved weekly;
- at least four durable evidence units are recorded;
- project resumption after a seven-day gap takes less than ten minutes;
- panel package review averages less than three minutes;
- ACE maintenance remains below 5% of planning/review time;
- Obsidian accurately marks pending, current, stale, or unknown states.

## Success after 90 days

At least one anchor or major project demonstrates two or more:

- submission or publication;
- external review;
- repeated reuse;
- accumulated validated cases or data;
- adoption by another person or team;
- continued operation during the operator's absence;
- successful ownership transfer;
- increased protected time, resources, or decision authority.

## Failure condition

ACE fails if it makes the operator more organized but does not reduce cognitive burden, preserve reliable routine execution, or create durable externalized value.

## Lineage guard *(added v1.1 — evidence: 2026-07-19 workspace research)*

ACE is at least the fifth meta-system in this lineage since Dec 2023 — four of them since Jan 2026. Every predecessor died at the maintenance step, exactly where the legacy PKM system's own review notes predicted. The design lesson is structural: any surface that needs routine upkeep stalls once the upkeep stops. The lineage:

| System | Built | Stalled | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| OneNote GTD+PARA (notebook-based) | Dec 2023–Feb 2024 | ~Mar 2024 (sporadic stragglers into mid-2025) | activity concentrated in the build months, then a trickle; review docs stop Mar 2024 |
| Legacy PKM vault (Obsidian migration) | Jan 2026 import | ~2 weeks later | a handful of notes edited post-import, none since |
| Documents-folder PARA reorg | Mar 2026 | same day | one-shot timestamps; no follow-up filing |
| Desktop-folder agent contract | Jun 2026 | drifted within 5 weeks | STATUS.md layer never updated; 2 un-indexed workstreams; rule 4 violated at root |
| Another meta-system + scheduled skills | 2026-04→07 | live | still active — coexistence unresolved |

Consequence for design *(APPROVED via R2-2 sign-off, 2026-07-19)*: treat the cognitive-burden limits in `03_PRODUCT_SPEC.md` as **hard failure gates, not aspirations**. Any ACE surface that requires routine human maintenance beyond those budgets places ACE on this list. When in doubt, delete the surface — the Red-Queen `Simplicity` evaluator (07) adjudicates.
